{{% callout info %}}
I have had excellent experiences hosting visiting students and am always happy to consider new visits to my lab.
I also welcome collaborations on topics related to my group's work.
Before reaching out, please review our current research and consider how your interests and proposed work align with it.
{{% /callout %}}

**Research visits.** Please [email me](mailto:thomas.pasquier@cs.ox.ac.uk) at least six months before your proposed visit with your CV, current affiliation, a brief research outline, proposed dates, and details of your secured funding.
Explain how the visit would connect with our research.

**Collaborations.** Please [email me](mailto:thomas.pasquier@cs.ox.ac.uk) with a brief description of the question or project you have in mind, how it relates to my research, and what you would like us to work on together.
