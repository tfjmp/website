I welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers interested in applying for a fellowship with my group at Oxford.
If your interests align with [my research](/research/), I would be happy to discuss developing a fellowship proposal together.
{{% /callout %}}

#### Developing a proposal

A fellowship is an opportunity to develop your own research ideas in collaboration with the group.
Before getting in touch, please read some of my [recent publications](/research/#publications) and consider how your proposed work connects with them.
I would be interested to hear about the questions you want to investigate, the experience you would bring, and the directions you would like to explore during the fellowship.

#### Getting in touch

Please [email me](mailto:thomas.pasquier@cs.ox.ac.uk) with:

- Your CV, including publications.
- A brief outline of your proposed research and how it connects with my group's work.
- The fellowship scheme or schemes you are considering, their deadlines, and how you meet their eligibility requirements.

Please get in touch several months before the application deadline.
This gives us time to discuss the project, develop the proposal, and check any departmental requirements before submission.

#### Fellowship schemes

Depending on your career stage and circumstances, schemes to consider include:

- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships), which support research and training across disciplines and have specific international mobility requirements.
- [Royal Society Newton International Fellowships](https://royalsociety.org/grants/newton-international/), for eligible early-career researchers based outside the UK who wish to conduct research here.
- [1851 Research Fellowships](https://royalcommission1851.org/fellowships/research-fellowships), which support independent research by early-career scientists and engineers.

Please check the scheme's current eligibility rules and deadlines before contacting me.
I am also happy to discuss other suitable fellowships you have identified.

#### College research fellowships

Oxford colleges offer **Junior Research Fellowships (JRFs)** and equivalent early-career research opportunities under other titles, such as [Post-Doctoral Research Fellowships at All Souls](https://www.asc.ox.ac.uk/join-the-college/post-doctoral-research-fellowships).
Eligible subjects, selection criteria, and application dates vary by college and competition.
See the [college academic vacancies](https://collegevacancies.site.ox.ac.uk/academic-vacancies) and [University Gazette](https://www.ox.ac.uk/about/how-we-are-run/gazette/jobs) for advertisements.

Check whether a fellowship is **stipendiary** (providing salary funding) or **non-stipendiary** (requiring separate salary funding).
For example, at Brasenose, the [Nicholas Kurti Junior Research Fellowship in the Sciences](https://www.bnc.ox.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Nicholas-Kurti-Junior-FP-2026.pdf) is non-stipendiary and requires a salaried research post at the University or an associated research institute.
If you are considering a college fellowship, please get in touch to discuss how your research could connect with my group.
