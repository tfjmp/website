I welcome inquiries from Oxford undergraduate and MSc students interested in projects related to my research.
Please follow the Department of Computer Science's guidance on choosing a project, contacting supervisors, and registering your topic.
{{% /callout %}}

#### Choosing a project

Start with the [department's student-project page](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/teaching/courses/projects/), which lists potential projects and supervisors and indicates suitability for Part B, Part C, and MSc students.

You can work on a project I have proposed or suggest your own project related to my research.

#### Contact and registration

Please [contact me](mailto:thomas.pasquier@cs.ox.ac.uk) during the supervisor-contact period specified by the department.
In your email, include your course and year of study, the project or topic that interests you, and a brief description of your relevant background.

Once we have agreed on a project, complete the department's registration process and any proposal required for your course.
Consult the departmental guidance and your course handbook for current deadlines, assessment requirements, and advice on writing your dissertation.
